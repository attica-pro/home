### 1. Demolition

![Bare walls after the tiles came off](step-01.jpg)

The old tiles and fittings come out, leaving the walls bare.

### 2. Building the shower area

![Low brickwork in the shower corner](step-02.jpg)

Low sections of brickwork are built where the shower will go.

### 3. New pipework

![New drain pipes in the floor](step-03.jpg)

The floor is opened up and new drain and water pipes are laid.

### 4. The floor

![The floor laid, with the drain in place](step-04.jpg)

The floor is laid again around the drains and made ready for tiling.

### 5. Tiles and niche

![Grey tiles, a wall niche and the shower step](step-05.jpg)

Walls and floor are covered with large grey tiles. A niche is left in the shower wall for bottles.

### 6. The fittings

![The new toilet in place](step-06.jpg)

The new toilet and the other fittings go in.

### 7. The result

![A new basin with a cabinet and mirror](step-07.jpg)

A basin with a cabinet, a mirror with a shelf and the same grey tile throughout. A bathroom that is easy to keep clean.
