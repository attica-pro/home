### 1. Primer

![A roof coated with black primer](step-01.jpg)

First a bitumen primer goes over the whole roof, so the foam bonds firmly.

### 2. Under the tanks

![Primer under the water heater and between its legs](step-02.jpg)

The primer also goes under the tanks, between their legs.

### 3. The feet

![Foam around the feet of a metal tank](step-03.jpg)

The tank feet are sprayed carefully, because they are awkward spots where leaks open easily.

### 4. Spraying the parapets

![A worker in a protective suit spraying foam on the parapet](step-04.jpg)

The foam also goes up the parapets, so the insulation rises well up the wall and there is no joint in the corner.

### 5. The corner

![Spraying foam into a corner of the roof](step-05.jpg)

The foam fills the corner between floor and wall and joins the two surfaces into one continuous layer.

### 6. Around the pipes

![Foam around a pipe and a metal tank](step-06.jpg)

Pipes, bases and walls are covered in the same foam, with no gaps in between.

### 7. Around the rooftop room

![The insulated roof around the rooftop room](step-07.jpg)

The insulation reaches the foot of the rooftop room and the edges of the roof.

### 8. The result

![The finished roof in light-coloured foam](step-08.jpg)

The whole roof is now covered. The foam insulates and waterproofs in one go, and keeps the home cooler in summer.
