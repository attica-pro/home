### 1. A rusted base

![A rusted steel post with torn membrane around it](step-01.jpg)

Around the rusted post the membrane had torn and come loose. Small openings like this are how water finds its way to the ceiling below.

### 2. Sealing the post

![The post with a square of white waterproofing at its base](step-02.jpg)

The spot gets a dark primer and then a white waterproof coating that wraps around the post and bonds to the membrane around it.

### 3. The base of a pipe

![A white pipe with its base sealed on the roof](step-03.jpg)

Every pipe coming out of the roof is sealed the same way, so there is no open joint left at its base.

### 4. The solar heater feet

![Solar water heater feet with white waterproofing](step-04.jpg)

The feet of the solar water heater stand on the membrane. Each one gets its own white square.

### 5. Where the roof meets the parapet

![Dark primer in the corner between the roof and the parapet](step-05.jpg)

The corner where the floor meets the parapet is another weak spot. First a dark primer goes along its whole length.

### 6. White waterproofing on the parapet

![The parapet with a white waterproof coat](step-06.jpg)

The white coating covers the corner and runs up the parapet, so rainwater flows over the joint instead of into it.
