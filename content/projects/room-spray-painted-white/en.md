### 1. Preparation

![Teal walls and furniture covered in plastic](step-01.jpg)

The furniture is covered with plastic sheeting. A spray gun puts paint mist in the air, so anything left uncovered would get speckled.

### 2. Spraying

![Spraying white paint onto the wall](step-02.jpg)

The paint goes on in thin, even layers, quickly and without roller marks.

### 3. The first coat

![The wall after the first white coat](step-03.jpg)

After the first coat the old colour still shows through in places, so another coat follows.

### 4. The result

![The white wall, with the wall light on](step-04.jpg)

The wall is evenly white, and the room looks brighter.
