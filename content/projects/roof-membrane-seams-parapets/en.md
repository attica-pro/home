### 1. A torn membrane

![A hand showing the torn membrane at the edge of the roof](step-01.jpg)

At the edge of the roof the old membrane had torn and come away, and water and dirt had collected underneath.

### 2. Signs of leaks

![A corner with dark water marks](step-02.jpg)

In the corners the marks left by water getting through were easy to see.

### 3. Removing the loose pieces

![Pieces of old membrane gathered on the roof](step-03.jpg)

The pieces of membrane that had come loose are cut out and taken away.

### 4. The foot of the parapet

![Repairs at the foot of the parapet under the railings](step-04.jpg)

At the foot of the parapet, under the railings, the old finish had gone. The area is cleaned and covered with a white waterproof coating.

### 5. All the way along

![The low wall covered with a white coat](step-05.jpg)

The white coat runs the full length of the parapet and closes the joint with the membrane.

### 6. The membrane seams

![White strips over the membrane seams](step-06.jpg)

Every seam between the sheets of membrane is sealed with a white strip. Seams are where water usually gets in.

### 7. The top of the parapet

![The top of the parapet coated white](step-07.jpg)

The top of the parapet, where the railings are fixed, gets the same material.

### 8. The result

![The roof with sealed seams and a white perimeter](step-08.jpg)

Seams and parapets closed up, with a white line all the way round the roof.
