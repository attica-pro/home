### 1. Damp low on the walls

![Bubbling paint above the skirting](step-01.jpg)

Damp was rising from below, and the paint above the skirting boards had bubbled and peeled.

### 2. Scraping

![A wall scraped back around the damaged areas](step-02.jpg)

Anything that has bubbled is scraped back to a sound surface. Otherwise the new paint comes off together with the old.

### 3. The collapsed ceiling

![A hole in the ceiling where the plaster fell](step-03.jpg)

In one room a section of ceiling plaster had come down, exposing the structure above.

### 4. Plastering and filling

![Patches on walls and ceiling before painting](step-04.jpg)

The ceiling is re-plastered and the walls are filled. The white areas are patches that get sanded before painting.

### 5. Protecting the space

![Furniture covered with plastic sheeting](step-05.jpg)

Furniture that stays in the room is covered before painting.

### 6. The result

![The painted hallway](step-06.jpg)

Clean walls and ceilings throughout the apartment.
