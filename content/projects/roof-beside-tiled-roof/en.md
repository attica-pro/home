### 1. The old membrane

![Torn old membrane under the solar panels](step-01.jpg)

The old membrane had torn and come away in large pieces. It was no longer protecting the roof.

### 2. Stripping

![Pieces of stripped membrane on the roof](step-02.jpg)

All of the membrane is stripped off, so the new work goes onto a sound base.

### 3. Primer

![The roof coated with black primer under the panels](step-03.jpg)

The surface gets a dark primer, including under the panels and around their bases.

### 4. Pouring the screed

![A worker spreading screed under the solar panels](step-04.jpg)

The screed is pumped up to the roof and spread starting from the hardest spots, under the panels.

### 5. Around the bases

![Fresh screed around the panel bases](step-05.jpg)

The screed flows around the solar panel bases and wraps them in. The panels stay in place throughout.

### 6. Beside the tiled roof

![A grey flat roof beside a tiled roof](step-06.jpg)

The flat roof meets the bottom edge of a tiled roof. That spot needs care, because water runs down onto it from the tiles.

### 7. Primer before the foam

![The roof coated with black primer](step-07.jpg)

Black primer goes over the new surface, up to the parapets and the edge of the tiles, so the foam bonds well.

### 8. Spray polyurethane foam

![Polyurethane foam in front of the tiles](step-08.jpg)

Spray polyurethane foam goes on in one continuous layer with no joints and closes the corner between the flat roof and the tiled roof.
