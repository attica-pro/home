### 1. The old bathroom

![The old bathroom with its bathtub](step-01.jpg)

A tired bathroom with old tiles, a bathtub and fittings that had had their day.

### 2. Demolition

![Stripping out the tiles and fittings](step-02.jpg)

Tiles, basin and toilet come out, and the walls are taken back to the brick.

### 3. New pipework

![New water pipes across the floor and walls](step-03.jpg)

The water pipes are replaced. The new pipes run through the floor and walls before they are closed up.

### 4. Built-in cistern

![The frame of the built-in cistern in place](step-04.jpg)

The cistern goes inside the wall. That saves space and makes the bathroom look tidier.

### 5. Rendering the walls

![The rendered walls with a scored surface](step-05.jpg)

The walls are rendered again and the surface is scored so the tile adhesive grips well.

### 6. The ceiling

![Plasterboard on the ceiling with filled joints](step-06.jpg)

The ceiling is closed in with plasterboard, which later takes recessed lights.

### 7. Tiling

![Striped tiles going up on the walls](step-07.jpg)

The striped tiles are laid carefully so the lines stay straight from wall to wall.

### 8. The result

![The finished bathroom with a new vanity unit](step-08.jpg)

A bright, modern bathroom with new tiles and a basin with a cabinet underneath.
