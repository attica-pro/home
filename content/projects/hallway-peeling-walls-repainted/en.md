### 1. Where we started

![A peeling patch high on the wall](step-01.jpg)

The paint had lifted in patches, even high up the wall.

### 2. By the stairs

![Peeling wall next to the door and stairs](step-02.jpg)

Next to the door and the stairs, too, the paint had come off down to the plaster.

### 3. Preparation

![The repaired wall with masking tape along the edges](step-03.jpg)

The damaged patches are scraped and filled, and the edges are masked off before painting.

### 4. Painting

![The wall next to the door, painted](step-04.jpg)

The new paint goes over whole walls, not just the patches, so there are no differences in shade.

### 5. The result

![The finished entrance](step-05.jpg)

Smooth walls in a warm cream, with no trace of the peeling.
