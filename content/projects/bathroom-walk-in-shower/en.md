### 1. The old bathroom

![The bathroom with dark old tiles and a tub](step-01.jpg)

Dark tiles that had started to come loose, a tub and an old toilet. The demolition has already begun.

### 2. Bare walls and a new drain

![The walls without tiles and the floor with a new drain](step-02.jpg)

The tiles and the tub come out. A spot opens in the floor for the new drain, and the new pipes are run.

### 3. A frame for a wall-hung toilet

![A concealed cistern frame fixed to the wall](step-03.jpg)

The frame with the concealed cistern is fixed to the wall. It will be hidden behind the tiles and hold the toilet off the floor, which makes the floor easy to clean.

### 4. A sloped floor

![The new floor with a channel drain under the window](step-04.jpg)

The new floor slopes towards a long, narrow channel drain. That way the shower sits level with the rest of the bathroom and the water drains away instead of spreading.

### 5. Tiling

![Tiles on the toilet wall, with the supply points sticking out](step-05.jpg)

Tiles cover the walls and floor. A built-in shelf is formed around the toilet frame.

### 6. The shower

![The shower with a glass panel and mixer](step-06.jpg)

A glass panel keeps the water inside the shower, with no doors and no step.

### 7. Basin and towel rail

![A basin on a wooden stand, with a mirror and a towel rail](step-07.jpg)

The basin sits on a wooden stand, with a mirror above it and a towel rail on the next wall.

### 8. The wall-hung toilet

![The wall-hung toilet with the flush plate on the wall](step-08.jpg)

The toilet hangs from the wall, and only the flush plate shows on the panel above it.
