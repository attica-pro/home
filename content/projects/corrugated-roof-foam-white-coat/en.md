### 1. Washing

![Pressure washing the old roof](step-01.jpg)

The old sheets were black with dirt. Pressure washing leaves a clean surface for the foam to stick to.

### 2. Spraying

![A worker spraying foam onto the corrugated sheets](step-02.jpg)

The foam is sprayed onto the sheets and follows their shape, covering the joints and screws.

### 3. White coat

![The foamed roof with the white coat starting on top](step-03.jpg)

A white protective coat goes over the foam, because unprotected foam wears out in the sun.

### 4. The result

![The finished roof, white along its whole length](step-04.jpg)

A roof with no open joints that reflects the heat instead of passing it into the space below.
