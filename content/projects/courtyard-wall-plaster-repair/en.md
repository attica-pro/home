### 1. The wall before

![A courtyard wall with peeling plaster behind railings](step-01.jpg)

The plaster had blistered and fallen off in large pieces, and the paint was peeling.

### 2. Removing and patching

![The wall with fresh plaster patches and mesh](step-02.jpg)

Anything loose comes off until we reach a sound base. The holes are filled with new plaster.

### 3. Mesh

![A roll of mesh next to the fresh patches](step-03.jpg)

Mesh goes over the patches and where they meet the old plaster, so cracks don't open up later.

### 4. A thin finishing coat

![The wall with an even finishing coat and the plasterer's float](step-04.jpg)

A thin coat of plaster goes over the whole wall, so the patches and the old parts become one even surface.

### 5. Painting

![The wall painted white behind the railings](step-05.jpg)

The wall is painted with an exterior paint that stands up to sun and rain.

### 6. The result

![The narrow courtyard passage with the wall finished](step-06.jpg)

The wall is clean and straight again along its whole length, and the courtyard feels brighter.
