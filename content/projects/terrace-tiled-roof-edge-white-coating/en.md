### 1. Where we started

![Torn old membrane sheets on the terrace](step-01.jpg)

The old membrane had torn and lifted in many places, leaving open joints.

### 2. Stripping

![Pulling up the old membrane below the roof tiles](step-02.jpg)

The old membrane comes off completely, including from the walls under the roof tiles.

### 3. Sheet by sheet

![Pulling a sheet of membrane off the terrace](step-03.jpg)

New material doesn't go on top of membrane that has come loose. Left in place, it would give the new coating no solid base.

### 4. A clean surface

![The terrace with the old membrane gone](step-04.jpg)

With the membrane gone, the surface is cleaned and checked before waterproofing.

### 5. Walls first

![White waterproofing on the wall under the roof tiles](step-05.jpg)

The white coating starts on the walls, right under the edge of the tiled roof.

### 6. The corner

![The corner of the walls in white, with the floor still dark](step-06.jpg)

Corners and the joint with the floor are covered carefully, as that's where cracks usually open.

### 7. The floor

![The terrace covered in white waterproofing](step-07.jpg)

The coating spreads over the whole floor and joins the walls into one continuous surface.

### 8. Finishing touches

![Rolling the last spots on the walls](step-08.jpg)

The last spots on the walls are rolled so no gap is left.
