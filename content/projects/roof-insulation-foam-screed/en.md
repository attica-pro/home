### 1. The bare slab

![The roof slab before the work](step-01.jpg)

The roof was plain concrete with no insulation at all, so in summer the heat goes straight into the home below.

### 2. Spraying begins

![A worker spraying the foam onto the slab](step-02.jpg)

Spray polyurethane foam is sprayed straight onto the slab and bonds to the whole surface, even around the steel bars sticking up.

### 3. The whole roof insulated

![The roof covered in white foam](step-03.jpg)

The foam forms one continuous layer with no joints, so there are no gaps where heat can get in or out.

### 4. A coat of tar

![Tar being spread over the foam](step-04.jpg)

A coat of tar goes over the foam to protect it before the screed is poured.

### 5. The concrete pump

![A concrete pump next to the roof](step-05.jpg)

The screed is pumped up to the roof, so it can be spread over the whole surface in one go.

### 6. The new screed

![Fresh screed across the whole roof](step-06.jpg)

The screed covers and protects the insulation and gives a solid surface that can be walked on.

### 7. Levelling

![A worker levelling the fresh screed with a straightedge](step-07.jpg)

The surface is levelled with a straightedge while it's still wet, so no hollows are left for water to pool in.
