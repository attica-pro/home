### 1. Protecting the room

![A room covered in plastic sheeting from ceiling to floor](step-01.jpg)

Walls, windows and everything in the room are covered in plastic. Spraying puts fine droplets in the air, and everything has to stay clean.

### 2. The ceiling before

![The ceiling before spraying, with the windows covered](step-02.jpg)

The ceiling is ready for spraying and everything below it is protected.

### 3. Spraying

![A worker spraying foam onto the ceiling](step-03.jpg)

Spray polyurethane foam goes over the whole surface and forms one continuous insulating layer with no joints.

### 4. The light fittings

![A light fitting wrapped in plastic on the sprayed ceiling](step-04.jpg)

The light fittings are wrapped so they stay clean, and the foam is sprayed carefully around them.

### 5. Painting

![A worker on a stepladder painting the ceiling white](step-05.jpg)

White paint goes over the foam, protecting it and making the ceiling bright.

### 6. White over the foam

![The ceiling half beige and half painted white](step-06.jpg)

Here you can see the difference: the beige foam and the white paint covering it.

### 7. The finished ceiling

![A lantern hanging under the white ceiling](step-07.jpg)

A white ceiling with a soft texture that keeps the room cooler in summer and warmer in winter.

### 8. The result

![The finished space, with the wooden lattice](step-08.jpg)

The plastic comes down and the space is ready again, now with an insulated ceiling.
