The stripped areas are filled with new plaster and brought level with the rest of the wall.

### 5. The counter area restored

![The wall behind the counter plastered and painted](step-05.jpg)

The wall behind the counter was plastered and painted white.

### 6. Next to the window

### 7. The same corner afterwards

### 8. The apartment ready

![The apartment entrance with freshly painted walls](step-08.jpg)
