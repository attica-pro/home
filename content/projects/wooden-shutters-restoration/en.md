### 1. The old shutters

![The shutters before, with worn paint](step-01.jpg)

The paint had peeled and the wood was left exposed to the weather, especially at the bottom.

### 2. Sanding

![Sanding a shutter with a power sander](step-02.jpg)

The old paint comes off with a sander and scraper until sound wood shows.

### 3. Primer

![The shutters primed](step-03.jpg)

The primer seals the grain of the wood and gives the final paint a good base.

### 4. The result

![The shutters painted and ready](step-04.jpg)

The same shutters, painted and protected for years to come.
