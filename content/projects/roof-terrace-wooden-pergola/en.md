### 1. The pergola

![A wooden slatted pergola seen from below](step-01.jpg)

The pergola was already there. Its wooden frame and the closely spaced slats that cut the sun were maintained and given fresh varnish.

### 2. The dining area

![A dining table under the pergola, with lattice screens around it](step-02.jpg)

The dining table sits under the pergola. The lattice screens around it were varnished too, so they stand up to the weather.

### 3. A second pergola

![Two wooden pergolas on the terrace, among plants](step-03.jpg)

The second pergola, in another corner of the terrace, got the same maintenance.

### 4. Planters

![A planter clad in wooden lattice](step-04.jpg)

The planters are clad in the same lattice and were varnished in the same dark tone, so the whole terrace has one consistent look.

### 5. The lattice up close

![A close-up of a wooden lattice panel](step-05.jpg)

The thin battens cross in a tight grid, and the varnish has to reach every one of them. It protects the wood from sun and rain.

### 6. Screens on the railings

![Lattice panels behind iron railings](step-06.jpg)

The tall panels behind the railings, which screen the terrace from the neighbouring buildings, take the full weather and need regular varnishing.

### 7. The walkway

![A covered walkway with a pergola leading to the door](step-07.jpg)

The covered walkway to the door, with its wooden floor and pergola overhead, after the maintenance.
