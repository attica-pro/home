### 1. The pump

![A pump hose pouring screed onto the roof](step-01.jpg)

The screed is pumped straight up onto the roof, so it can be spread quickly over the whole surface.

### 2. Spreading

![A worker spreading the screed](step-02.jpg)

The material is spread and levelled across the roof. A level, solid base is the first step to good insulation.

### 3. The new base

![The roof with the new screed dried](step-03.jpg)

Once the screed has dried, the roof has one continuous base for the next layers.

### 4. Bitumen primer

![The roof coated with black primer](step-04.jpg)

A bitumen primer goes over the whole surface. It binds the dust and helps the next material stick firmly.

### 5. Around the solar heater

![Primer around the solar water heater and the steel bars](step-05.jpg)

The primer reaches everywhere: around the solar heater, the protruding steel bars and the foot of the parapets.

### 6. Spraying the foam

![A worker spraying polyurethane foam over the primer](step-06.jpg)

The polyurethane foam is sprayed over the primer and expands into one continuous layer with no joints.

### 7. The result

![The foam covering the roof](step-07.jpg)

The foam insulates and waterproofs in one go. The home below is protected from rain and from the summer heat.
