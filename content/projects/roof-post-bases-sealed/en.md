### 1. The old seal had cracked

![A cracked old seal at the base of a post](step-01.jpg)

At the base of every metal post the old seal had cracked and come loose. That is where water finds its way under the membrane.

### 2. Cleaning and a first coat

![The first white coat at the base of the post](step-02.jpg)

Once the loose material is off, the base is covered with a white waterproof coating.

### 3. Reinforcing the base

![Reinforcing fabric set into the white coat](step-03.jpg)

Reinforcing fabric is set into the wet material, which binds the coating together and keeps it in one piece.

### 4. Up onto the metal

![The coating taken up the post](step-04.jpg)

The coating is also taken up the post, because the joint between metal and roof is the weakest point.

### 5. A wider layer of fabric

![A large piece of fabric around the post](step-05.jpg)

A wider piece of fabric covers the whole base and reaches past the old dark strip.

### 6. Final coat

![The base covered with the final white coat](step-06.jpg)

The white material goes over the fabric and covers the whole square.

### 7. Every post

![Several posts with white bases across the roof](step-07.jpg)

The same work is done at every base, so no weak point is left on the roof.
