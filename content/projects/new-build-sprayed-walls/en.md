### 1. Bare walls

![A building with bare grey outside walls](step-01.jpg)

A house under construction, with its outside walls still grey and unprotected.

### 2. The scaffolding

![The face of the building with scaffolding before spraying](step-02.jpg)

With the scaffolding in place, the whole height of the wall can be sprayed.

### 3. The polyurethane foam

![The wall covered in cream polyurethane foam](step-03.jpg)

Spray polyurethane foam goes over the whole surface and forms one continuous cream layer, carried around the openings as well.

### 4. Ready for render

![The building with its walls covered in foam](step-04.jpg)

The whole face is insulated, with no joints or gaps. Render then goes over the foam to protect it and give the walls their final finish.
