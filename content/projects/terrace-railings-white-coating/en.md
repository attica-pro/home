### 1. Where we started

![The old terrace with worn paving](step-01.jpg)

The paving was worn and stained. The darker patch in the middle shows where the work began.

### 2. Base layer

![The terrace with its base layer, where the white starts](step-02.jpg)

A base layer covers the whole surface and evens out the paving and its joints. At the far end the white coat is already starting.

### 3. The white coating

![The white coating across the terrace](step-03.jpg)

The white waterproof coating is spread across the whole terrace, right up to the foot of the low walls.

### 4. One continuous surface

![The terrace with the tins of material](step-04.jpg)

The surface is now one piece, with none of the old paving joints showing. The white also reflects the sun.

### 5. The details

![The white surface around the base of a satellite dish](step-05.jpg)

The coating also goes around the base of the satellite dish, so no spot is left bare.
