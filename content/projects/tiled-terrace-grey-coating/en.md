### 1. Loose tiles

![Loose tiles stacked on the terrace](step-01.jpg)

Many tiles had come loose and were taken up, leaving patches of old adhesive underneath. Water easily finds its way in through spots like these.

### 2. Peeling old coatings

![Tiles with an old white coating peeling off](step-02.jpg)

Elsewhere an old white coating had lifted and was only holding in places. Anything loose comes off.

### 3. Washing and sealing the joints

![The washed terrace, with white lines along the sealed joints](step-03.jpg)

The terrace is washed and the open joints and cracks are sealed, which is what the white lines are.

### 4. Filling the holes

![White patches over holes in the surface](step-04.jpg)

Small holes and broken spots are filled one by one before the waterproofing goes on.

### 5. Spraying

![A worker spraying the waterproofing with a hose](step-05.jpg)

The waterproofing is sprayed on with a hose. It gets into every joint and pore and goes up the foot of the walls.

### 6. Another coat

![The blue-grey terrace with another coat going on](step-06.jpg)

An extra coat builds up the thickness and covers anything that was missed.

### 7. The edges

![A roller on a pole and a tin of material by the wall](step-07.jpg)

The corners and the edges along the walls are done with a roller, since that's usually where the first cracks open.

### 8. The result

![The finished terrace in blue-grey waterproofing](step-08.jpg)

One continuous surface with no open joints, protecting the flats below.
