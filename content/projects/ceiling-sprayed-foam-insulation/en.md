### 1. The pilotis before

![The smooth pilotis ceiling before spraying](step-01.jpg)

The ceiling of the pilotis, the open ground floor under the building, is the floor of the flat above. Without insulation, cold and heat from the open space pass straight into the home. The light fitting has been taken down and its wiring wrapped.

### 2. Covering up

![Walls, openings and the air conditioning unit covered in plastic](step-02.jpg)

Walls, openings, the air conditioning unit and the floor are covered in plastic sheeting, so the foam goes only where it should.

### 3. Spraying

![The ceiling covered in foam, with the light fitting point protected](step-03.jpg)

Polyurethane foam is sprayed from below over the whole ceiling in one continuous layer. The light fitting point stays covered so the light can go back afterwards.

### 4. The surface

![Close-up of the foam surface](step-04.jpg)

One continuous layer with no joints, which cuts down the heat lost through the floor of the home into the open space below.

### 5. Around the lights

![A ceiling spotlight set into the foam](step-05.jpg)

Spotlights and wiring points are left free and clean.

### 6. The result

![The whole pilotis ceiling after spraying](step-06.jpg)

The whole pilotis ceiling is insulated, and the flat above now has a warmer floor in winter.
