### 1. During the painting

The lower part of the walls was painted grey-blue, with a thin line separating it from the lighter colour above, as suits an older house.

### 2. The doors

![A grey glazed double door next to a light wall](step-02.jpg)

The grey wooden doors with their glass panes tie in with the band on the wall.

### 3. The bedroom

![A bedroom with lilac walls and tall windows](step-03.jpg)

In the bedroom the walls got a soft lilac that shows off the tall windows.

### 4. The kitchen

![A kitchen with a two-tone wall and white cupboards](step-04.jpg)

In the kitchen the wall is split into two tones, with the darker colour at the bottom, where it gets dirty more easily.

### 5. The staircase

![An internal staircase with grey steps](step-05.jpg)

The internal staircase has grey steps and light walls, in the same tones as the rest of the house.

### 6. The ceiling

![A ceiling with a white decorative frame](step-06.jpg)

On the ceiling, the old decorative frame is white and stands out from the colour around it.

### 7. The hallway

![A hallway with doors and an old patterned tile floor](step-07.jpg)

From room to room, the same few colours repeat and pull the whole house together.

### 8. The result
