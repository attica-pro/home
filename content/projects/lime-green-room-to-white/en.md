### 1. Where we started

The walls were a loud lime green. The white spots are filler in the holes and cracks.

### 2. Filling

![Filler along corners and joints in the wall](step-02.jpg)

Cracks in the corners and joints are filled. The smoother the base, the cleaner the final paint.

### 3. The old floor tiles

![Broken old tiles on the floor](step-03.jpg)

The broken floor tiles were lifted before the painting started.

### 4. White walls

![The room painted white, with the glazed door](step-04.jpg)

Covering such a strong colour takes several coats of white, so no green shows through.

### 5. Door and ceiling

![A white wall with a door and ceiling lights](step-05.jpg)

Walls and ceiling in the same clean white.

### 6. The result

![A white wall with glass blocks](step-06.jpg)

A bright, neutral room, ready to be furnished however the owner likes.
