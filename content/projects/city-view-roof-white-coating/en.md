### 1. The roof before

![The dark surface of the roof](step-01.jpg)

The old dark coating was worn out, shiny in some places and frayed in others.

### 2. Damage at the railing

![Broken coating at the base of the railing](step-02.jpg)

At the base of the railing the coating had broken and a hole had opened. Spots like this are repaired first.

### 3. Across the whole surface

![The roof with the air-conditioning unit](step-03.jpg)

The wear covered the whole roof, even around the equipment.

### 4. The parapet

![The worn parapet with its railing](step-04.jpg)

The parapet had also gone black and frayed. The waterproofing has to go up there too, or water gets in at the edges.

### 5. Details first

![The base of the railing covered in white waterproofing](step-05.jpg)

Corners, railing bases and joints are covered first, because that is where leaks usually start.

### 6. White waterproofing

![Rolling on the white waterproof coating](step-06.jpg)

The white waterproof coating is rolled out over the whole surface.

### 7. Nearly done

![The roof almost completely white](step-07.jpg)

The coating reaches the edges and runs up the parapets.

### 8. The result

![The white roof with the air-conditioning unit](step-08.jpg)

One continuous white roof. The white reflects the sun, so the flat below heats up less.
