### 1. Damp stains

![A wall with black damp stains](step-01.jpg)

The terrace walls had black stains and worn paint from the damp.

### 2. Cracked parapets

![A parapet with cracks and peeling paint](step-02.jpg)

On the parapets the render had cracked and the paint was peeling. Water gets into the wall through the cracks.

### 3. Removing loose render

![A parapet with the render removed and the bricks exposed](step-03.jpg)

Render that was no longer holding was taken off down to the bricks. Nothing holds on a crumbling base.

### 4. New render

![Fresh render on the lower part of the parapet](step-04.jpg)

The bare patches are rendered again with new mortar.

### 5. Smoothing

![A parapet with a repaired, even surface](step-05.jpg)

The repairs are smoothed into the rest of the wall so they will not show after painting.

### 6. Painted walls

![The terrace with painted walls and primer starting on the floor](step-06.jpg)

The walls were painted a warm beige. On the floor the primer goes on, helping the waterproofing to bond.

### 7. White waterproofing

![The terrace floor with white waterproofing](step-07.jpg)

The white waterproofing covers the whole floor and runs up the foot of the walls.

### 8. The result

![The terrace with a bench under the awning](step-08.jpg)

A well-kept terrace with sound walls and a watertight floor, ready to be used again.
