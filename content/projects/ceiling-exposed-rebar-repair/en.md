### 1. The damage

![The ceiling with its plaster gone, stained concrete and visible bars](step-01.jpg)

The plaster had fallen off, leaving the concrete and its steel bars exposed.

### 2. Protecting the bars

![The ceiling bars painted with a red protective coat](step-02.jpg)

Every exposed bar gets a red protective coat so it does not keep rusting.

### 3. Building it back

![Repair mortar spread over the bars](step-03.jpg)

The missing surface is built back up with repair mortar, which covers and seals in the bars.

### 4. Filling and smoothing

![The repaired ceiling before painting](step-04.jpg)

The surface is filled and smoothed so the repaired patch doesn't stand out from the rest of the ceiling.

### 5. The result

![The painted ceiling](step-05.jpg)

One even, clean ceiling with no trace of the damage.
