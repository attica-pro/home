### 1. Washing

![Pressure washing the old paved roof](step-01.jpg)

The old paved surface is pressure washed to remove the dust and dirt that would stop the foam from bonding.

### 2. The whole surface

![The cleaned roof, with the paving joints showing](step-02.jpg)

The whole roof is cleaned, right up to the railings.

### 3. The machine

![The polyurethane spray machine](step-03.jpg)

The machine feeds the spray gun with the two materials that turn into foam when they meet.

### 4. Spraying

![Spraying polyurethane foam in a protective suit](step-04.jpg)

The foam is sprayed over the paving and expands almost at once into one continuous insulating layer. The sprayer wears a protective suit and mask.

### 5. Around the machinery

![The insulated surface around a ventilation unit](step-05.jpg)

The foam also wraps the bases of the ventilation units.

### 6. Under the solar heaters

![The insulated roof under a solar water heater](step-06.jpg)

The same goes for the legs of the solar water heaters, which are often where water gets in.

### 7. The result

![The finished roof, with its light-coloured surface](step-07.jpg)

One continuous, light-coloured surface across the whole roof. It insulates the building and leaves no joints for water.
