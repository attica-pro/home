### 1. Opening up the base

![Broken tiles and rubble around the base of a column](step-01.jpg)

The tiles and old bedding around the column are broken out and removed to expose its base.

### 2. Cleaning

![Cleaning the base with a pressure washer](step-02.jpg)

Dust and leftovers are washed away with a pressure washer, so the materials bond to a clean surface.

### 3. Ready for waterproofing

![The column base cleaned, with an open channel around it](step-03.jpg)

A clean channel is left all round the column, ready for the waterproofing.

### 4. The materials

![A roll of reinforcing fabric and a bucket of waterproofing](step-04.jpg)

The waterproofing is a liquid coating with a reinforcing fabric, which adds strength at the corner where the column meets the floor.

### 5. The waterproof layer

![A grey waterproof layer around the base of the column](step-05.jpg)

The layer covers the bottom of the channel and runs up the column, closing the corner with no joint.

### 6. At every column

![A sealed column base among the floor tiles](step-06.jpg)

The same work is done at every column on the terrace.

### 7. Along the foot of the walls

![An open channel along the foot of a wall](step-07.jpg)

A strip is opened along the foot of the wall too, as the joint between wall and floor is just as vulnerable.

### 8. The result

![The foot of the wall sealed with grey waterproofing](step-08.jpg)

The strip is sealed with the same system, so the terrace is closed off wherever it meets an upright surface.
