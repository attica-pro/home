### 1. The planter before

![The planter full of plants and cut branches](step-01.jpg)

The built-in planter on the terrace held soil and plants. Once the waterproofing underneath fails, water from watering seeps into the building.

### 2. Emptying it

![The empty planter, with its old black walls](step-02.jpg)

The plants and soil come out so the base can be seen. The old black coating is visible on the walls.

### 3. A clean base

![The planter floor cleaned](step-03.jpg)

The floor is cleared of soil and loose material, so the new layer bonds to a sound surface.

### 4. First layer

![A wet grey layer on the floor](step-04.jpg)

A first grey layer goes on to prepare the surface for the waterproofing.

### 5. Into the corners

![The first layer reaching the walls](step-05.jpg)

The layer runs right up to the foot of the walls, because leaks usually start at the corners.

### 6. White waterproofing

![The planter coated white on the floor and walls](step-06.jpg)

The white waterproofing covers the floor and goes up the walls, so the planter becomes a closed basin that keeps the water in.
