### 1. Where we started

![The office wall with peeling paint around the window](step-01.jpg)

Paint had peeled around and below the window, a sign of damp.

### 2. Up close

![Peeling patches beside the window](step-02.jpg)

Where the damp had got in, the paint and the top layer of plaster had come away in large patches.

### 3. Scraping

![The scraped wall, with the loose paint gone](step-03.jpg)

Anything loose is scraped off. Paint over it and it will come away again, taking the new paint with it.

### 4. Filling

![Filled patches on the wall](step-04.jpg)

Dips and edges are filled so the wall is flat again.

### 5. Base coat

![The wall around the window with its base coat](step-05.jpg)

A base coat goes over the repaired areas and evens out the surface before the final paint.

### 6. Across the whole wall

![The wall under the window with its base coat](step-06.jpg)

The base coat is spread across the whole wall under the window, down to the skirting.

### 7. Painting

![The wall painted white](step-07.jpg)

The final paint gives an even, clean surface.

### 8. The result

![The finished wall by the window](step-08.jpg)

Where the blisters and peeling used to be, there is now a smooth, bright wall.
