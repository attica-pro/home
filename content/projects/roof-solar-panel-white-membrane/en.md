### 1. Grinding

![A grinder on the roof, with a patch already ground back](step-01.jpg)

The old, worn surface is ground back. Loose material comes off and leaves a clean base for the membrane to bond to.

### 2. Around the solar heater frame

![The roof under the solar water heater frame](step-02.jpg)

The preparation also covers the ground between the legs of the frame, which is hard to reach but often where water pools.

### 3. Primer

![Bottles of primer on the roof](step-03.jpg)

The primer binds the remaining dust and helps the membrane stick firmly to the concrete.

### 4. Low walls first

![The low wall coated white, with the bucket of material](step-04.jpg)

The white membrane goes on the low walls and corners first, since they are the most sensitive spots.

### 5. The result

![The roof bright white under the solar heater](step-05.jpg)

The whole roof gets one continuous white layer, even under the solar heater frame. The white reflects the sun and keeps the home below cooler.
