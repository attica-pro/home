### 1. Before

![A turquoise wall above the worktop](step-01.jpg)

The walls above the worktop were turquoise, with stains and wear.

### 2. Wear

![A worn wall next to the blue cupboard](step-02.jpg)

Around the cabinets the paint had come off in places and the wall was uneven.

### 3. Filling

![Wall and ceiling filled](step-03.jpg)

Walls and ceiling are filled to close cracks and holes and make the surface smooth.

### 4. Preparation

![Filled walls above the windows](step-04.jpg)

The filled areas are sanded so they won’t show through the paint.

### 5. Around the cabinets

![Preparing the walls around the cabinets](step-05.jpg)

The surfaces around and behind the cabinets are prepared too, so the paint reaches everywhere.

### 6. New colours

![The kitchen with grey lower walls and white above](step-06.jpg)

The lower part of the walls is painted light grey and the upper part white. The same cabinets look fresher.

### 7. The tall cupboard

![The tall cupboard painted grey](step-07.jpg)

The tall cupboard, which was blue, was painted the same grey as the walls.

### 8. The result

![The glazed door and the window after painting](step-08.jpg)

The door with the decorative glass was painted grey as well. The kitchen now looks bright and consistent.
