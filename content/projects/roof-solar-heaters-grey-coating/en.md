### 1. Where we started

![The roof with dirt and cables under the solar heaters](step-01.jpg)

The roof was covered in dirt, loose material and cables. The heater frames and the air conditioning unit get in the way, and those are exactly the spots that tend to get missed.

### 2. Cleaning

![The roof washed and still wet](step-02.jpg)

The surface is cleaned and washed so we can see what needs repairing.

### 3. Patching

![A damaged spot patched in the middle of the roof](step-03.jpg)

Damaged spots are filled and patched, so no holes are left under the waterproofing.

### 4. Material for levelling

![A pile of levelling material next to the air conditioning unit](step-04.jpg)

The roof had hollows where water pooled. The material is mixed to fill them.

### 5. A level surface

![The roof with a fresh levelling layer](step-05.jpg)

With the new layer the surface is level and water no longer sits on it.

### 6. Primer

![A tin of primer on the roof, with the air conditioning unit raised on bricks](step-06.jpg)

Primer goes on so the waterproofing bonds well. The air conditioning unit is raised on bricks so the spot underneath can be covered too.

### 7. First coat

![White waterproofing on the floor and the low wall](step-07.jpg)

The waterproofing is worked around every leg of the frames and up the low walls.

### 8. The result

![The roof coated grey under the solar heaters](step-08.jpg)

One continuous grey surface, with no bare spots between or under the frames.
