### 1. Preparation

![A bedroom with a poster on the wall and the furniture covered](step-01.jpg)

The furniture is covered in plastic before anything starts, because repairing a ceiling makes dust.

### 2. The crack in the ceiling

![A ceiling with an opened-up crack and patches](step-02.jpg)

The ceiling had cracks and blistered plaster. The loose pieces are removed and the spots are patched.

### 3. Rusted steel

![Rusted reinforcing steel inside the crack](step-03.jpg)

Inside the crack was rusted steel. Rusting steel swells and breaks the concrete around it, which is why the crack is opened up that far, so it can be repaired properly.

### 4. The ceiling finished

![The painted ceiling with its light fitting](step-04.jpg)

After the repair and painting, the ceiling is smooth and you can no longer see where the crack was.

### 5. The walls

![A painted wall with a floor lamp](step-05.jpg)

The walls were painted in a soft tone that goes with the ceiling.

### 6. The result

![The repainted bedroom with clean walls](step-06.jpg)

A clean, fresh room with no cracks or stains.
