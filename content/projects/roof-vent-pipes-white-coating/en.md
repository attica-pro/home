### 1. The worn coating

![The roof with its dirty, worn white coating](step-01.jpg)

The old white coating had turned grey and worn through in many places, especially where water used to sit.

### 2. Washing and a first coat

![The washed roof, with the base of the vent pipe already coated](step-02.jpg)

The surface is washed to get rid of dust and dirt. The bases of the vent pipes and posts are coated first, because joints open up easily there.

### 3. New waterproofing

![New white waterproofing on the roof, around the vent pipes](step-03.jpg)

The white waterproofing is spread over the whole surface and wraps around every pipe coming out of the roof.

### 4. Second coat

![A roller on a pole and a tin of material on the roof](step-04.jpg)

The second coat builds up the final thickness and covers the spots that still looked worn.

### 5. The low walls

![The low roof wall coated white on the inside and top](step-05.jpg)

The waterproofing goes up the low walls and over their tops, so water can't get in at the edges.

### 6. The result

![The finished roof, bright white](step-06.jpg)

A clean white surface that keeps the rain out and reflects the sun, so the space below heats up less.
