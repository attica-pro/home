### 1. Where we started

![The old pipe cover opened up and falling apart](step-01.jpg)

Pipes run along the foot of the wall. Their old cover had come apart and the material inside had soaked up water.

### 2. A wooden cover

![A new wooden box over the pipes](step-02.jpg)

The pipes are boxed in with new wood boards, following their run along the wall.

### 3. Sealing the joints

![White sealant at the joints with the wall and floor](step-03.jpg)

The joints with the wall and floor, and the box's own corners, are sealed first.

### 4. Every edge

![The box with its sealed corners, from another side](step-04.jpg)

Every edge is checked so the waterproofing that follows finds no open spot.

### 5. The result

![The box and the tiles around it covered in white waterproofing](step-05.jpg)

The box and a strip of the surrounding tiles are covered in white waterproofing. The pipes are now protected, and water finds no way in at the joints.
