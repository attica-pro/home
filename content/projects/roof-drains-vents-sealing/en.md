### 1. Worn-out drains

![A rusty, worn roof drain](step-01.jpg)

Around the old drains the material had rotted and rusted, so water was getting into the slab easily.

### 2. The opening in the parapet

![A drainage opening at the foot of a parapet](step-02.jpg)

In this corner the water leaves through an opening at the foot of the parapet. The area is cleaned before anything goes on.

### 3. First coat

![White waterproofing around the opening](step-03.jpg)

The white waterproofing is spread around the opening and carried inside it.

### 4. Reinforcing with fabric

![Reinforcing fabric around the opening](step-04.jpg)

Reinforcing fabric is laid into the wet material, following the shape of the opening, so the coating does not crack at the corners.

### 5. The base of a vent

![A piece of black membrane at the base of a vent](step-05.jpg)

The base of the vent pipe is first closed with a piece of membrane.

### 6. Mesh around the pipe

![Reinforcing mesh around the vent pipe](step-06.jpg)

Reinforcing mesh goes on top and wraps around the pipe.

### 7. Coating the pipe

![White waterproofing on the base and up the pipe](step-07.jpg)

The white material covers the base and runs up the pipe, so water cannot find a way in at the joint.

### 8. The vent finished

![The vent pipe with its finished white base](step-08.jpg)

A white, reinforced base, bonded to the rest of the roof.
