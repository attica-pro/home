### 1. Scaffolding and netting

![The building covered in scaffolding and blue netting](step-01.jpg)

Scaffolding goes up the full height, with safety netting so nothing falls below.

### 2. Damaged render

![Crumbling render on the edge of the wall](step-02.jpg)

Along the edges and corners the render had blown and was crumbling.

### 3. Scraping

![A worker scraping loose material off the wall](step-03.jpg)

Anything loose or hollow-sounding is scraped off until a sound surface is reached.

### 4. Filling

![The wall with white patches of filler](step-04.jpg)

The bare areas are filled so the wall is smooth again before painting.

### 5. Painting level by level

![A painted wall behind the scaffolding](step-05.jpg)

The painting moves from one scaffold level to the next, so the whole face gets an even finish.

### 6. The result

![The side wall of the building with the scaffolding gone](step-06.jpg)

With the scaffolding down, the side wall looks even and clean, with no cracks or patches showing.
