### 1. The old bathroom

Patterned tiles from another era, a green bathtub and pipework that had done its time.

### 2. Demolition

![Tiles coming off the wall](step-02.jpg)

The tiles, the bathtub and the old render all come off until the brick shows.

### 3. New pipework

With the walls open, new water and drain pipes go in, routed to where the new fixtures will sit.

### 4. Floor

![The new floor with the shower drain](step-04.jpg)

A new floor sloped toward the drain, so shower water runs away instead of pooling.

### 5. Rendering

![The walls rendered again](step-05.jpg)

The walls are rendered again and made straight, ready for the final coat.

### 6. A final coat of render

![The walls with their final coat of render before tiling](step-06.jpg)

A last coat of render leaves the walls smooth and straight, so the tiles sit properly.

### 7. Tiles and fixtures

![The new wall and floor tiles](step-07.jpg)

Large wall tiles, a dark floor and a feature wall in the shower area.

### 8. The shower

![The shower with its glass panel and heated towel rail](step-08.jpg)
