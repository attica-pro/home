### 1. Demolition

![The kitchen during demolition, with broken tiles on the floor](step-01.jpg)

The old tiles and units come out. Behind them are the old pipes and the damaged parts of the wall.

### 2. New supply points

![The bare walls with new water and electrical points](step-02.jpg)

The water and electrical points go where the new kitchen needs them, and the openings in the wall are closed up.

### 3. Walls ready

![The wall plastered and painted white](step-03.jpg)

The walls are plastered, made straight and painted. The new units will go up against a flat, clean surface with the supply points right where they should be.
