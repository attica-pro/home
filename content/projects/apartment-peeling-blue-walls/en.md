### 1. Where we started

![A blue wall with a poster glued onto it](step-01.jpg)

Blue walls throughout the home, and in one room a large poster glued straight onto the wall.

### 2. Paint coming away

![A wall where the blue paint has come off in large pieces](step-02.jpg)

The blue paint had lost its grip and was peeling off in sheets, exposing the old layers underneath. Paint over it and it comes off together with the new paint.

### 3. Scraping

![A hallway with half-scraped walls](step-03.jpg)

Anything that is not firmly attached is scraped off, in the rooms, the hallways and around the door frames.

### 4. Where the poster was

![A pale rectangle where the poster used to be](step-04.jpg)

The poster came off and left a rectangular mark. That is cleaned and smoothed too, so it will not show under the new paint.

### 5. Filling

![Filler around sockets and holes in the wall](step-05.jpg)

Holes and marks around sockets and switches are filled.

### 6. The first white coat

![The first coat of white next to the front door](step-06.jpg)

The white starts to cover the blue, one wall at a time.

### 7. Rooms finished

![A white room with a wardrobe and wooden floor](step-07.jpg)

White, smooth walls with no trace of the old damage.

### 8. The result

![An empty white room, ready to use](step-08.jpg)

A bright home with one colour throughout, ready to live in.
