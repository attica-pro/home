### 1. An open joint

![An open joint with dirt at the foot of the parapet](step-01.jpg)

Where the floor meets the parapet with the railings, the joint had opened and soil and leaves had collected in it.

### 2. Dirt and rust

![Blackened grout and rust at the foot of the railings](step-02.jpg)

Along the parapet the grout had turned black and the railings were rusting at the base.

### 3. Cleaning

![The terrace cleaned before sealing](step-03.jpg)

The terrace is cleaned and the joints are cleared of everything that had collected in them.

### 4. The materials

![Tins of waterproofing, a roll of fabric and tools](step-04.jpg)

White waterproofing, a roll of reinforcing fabric and the tools for the job.

### 5. Sealing the joint

![White waterproofing over the joint between floor and parapet](step-05.jpg)

The joint is covered with white waterproofing reinforced with fabric, taken up onto the low wall as well.

### 6. Along the parapet

![The parapet and railings along the terrace](step-06.jpg)

The work carries on along the whole length of the parapet, under the railings.

### 7. The result

![The terrace with its edges sealed](step-07.jpg)

A clean terrace, with the joints at the parapets closed to water.
