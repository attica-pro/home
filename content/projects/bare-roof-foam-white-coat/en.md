### 1. A bare slab

![A bare concrete roof with steel bars sticking up](step-01.jpg)

A roof of bare concrete, with no protection from sun or rain.

### 2. The low walls

![The low wall at the edge of the roof](step-02.jpg)

The low walls around the edge will be covered too, so the joint with the floor is not left open.

### 3. The foam

![The roof covered with beige polyurethane foam](step-03.jpg)

The whole surface is sprayed with polyurethane foam, which forms one continuous insulating layer with no joints.

### 4. Up the low walls

![The foam carried up the low walls of the roof](step-04.jpg)

The foam runs up the low walls and over their tops.

### 5. White protection

![A white coating rolled over the foam](step-05.jpg)

The foam must not be left exposed to the sun, so a white protective coating is rolled over it.

### 6. Across the whole surface

![The white coating gradually covering the foam](step-06.jpg)

The coating works its way across the whole roof, so no spot is left uncovered.

### 7. A fresh coat

![The wet white coating shining in the sun](step-07.jpg)

Freshly applied, the coating shines in the sun.

### 8. The result

![The roof all white, low walls included](step-08.jpg)

One continuous white roof. The foam insulates and the white reflects the sun, so the rooms below stay cooler.
