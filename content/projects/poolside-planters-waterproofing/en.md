### 1. The empty planter

![The planter floor with its old dark waterproofing](step-01.jpg)

With the soil out, the old dark waterproofing on the floor came into view, worn and patchy.

### 2. Removing the old layer

![Pieces of the old waterproofing piled in a corner](step-02.jpg)

The old layer is stripped out, so the new one goes onto a sound base rather than something already coming loose.

### 3. Preparation

![The planter floor cleaned and evened out](step-03.jpg)

The floor is cleaned and evened out, along with the corner where it meets the sides.

### 4. The long planter

![The long planter beside the house wall, emptied](step-04.jpg)

The long planter beside the house wall is emptied and cleaned the same way.

### 5. The new coating

![A grey waterproof coating on the floor and sides](step-05.jpg)

The waterproof coating covers the floor and runs up the sides, so watering doesn't soak into the walls.

### 6. Around the manhole

![White coating around a manhole with a grate](step-06.jpg)

A manhole inside the planter is covered on every side too.

### 7. The drainage holes

![The waterproofed planter, with its drainage holes left open](step-07.jpg)

The drainage holes are left open, so water runs away instead of sitting in the soil.

### 8. The result

![The waterproofed planters beside the pool](step-08.jpg)

The planters are ready to be filled with soil and planted again.
