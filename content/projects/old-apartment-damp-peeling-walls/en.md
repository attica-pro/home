### 1. Damp at the base of the walls

![Blown plaster low on the wall and paint flakes on the floor](step-01.jpg)

Low on the walls the damp had blown the plaster and the paint was falling onto the floor.

### 2. Scraping back to a sound surface

![The wall scraped with filled patches](step-02.jpg)

The loose paint and plaster are removed and the gaps are filled.

### 3. White again

![The wall by the window painted](step-03.jpg)

The wall painted and even from ceiling to floor.

### 4. Paint coming away

![Large patches of paint come away from the wall](step-04.jpg)

In another room, old layers of paint had come away in large patches.

### 5. Work by hand

![A worker on a ladder scraping paint with a scraper](step-05.jpg)

Every loose piece is scraped off by hand. Leave even a little and the new paint will peel off with it.

### 6. Mould on the ceiling

![A ceiling blackened by mould around a pipe](step-06.jpg)

Around the water heater pipe the ceiling had turned black with mould.

### 7. A clean ceiling

![The ceiling clean and painted around the pipe](step-07.jpg)

The mould is cleaned off before painting, otherwise it comes back through the new paint.

### 8. The apartment ready

![A freshly painted room with the doors open](step-08.jpg)

Freshly painted walls and door frames throughout the home.
