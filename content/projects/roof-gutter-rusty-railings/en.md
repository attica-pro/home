### 1. The narrow ledge

![A narrow strip of roof along the railings](step-01.jpg)

Along the railings, a narrow strip of roof with old, worn concrete.

### 2. The water channel

![A grey waterproofing layer inside the channel](step-02.jpg)

A grey waterproofing layer goes into the channel that collects the water first, filling the cracks.

### 3. All the way along

![The channel covered up to the outlet](step-03.jpg)

The layer runs the whole length of the channel, right up to the outlet.

### 4. The outlet

![White coating around the outlet opening](step-04.jpg)

Around the outlet the material is carried into the opening, so water drains away without finding a joint.

### 5. A white finish

![The strip of roof with the white coating](step-05.jpg)

A white coating goes on top, reflecting the sun and protecting the layer underneath.

### 6. Around the railings

![White coating at the feet of the railings](step-06.jpg)

The feet of the railings are covered carefully, because every fixing point is a possible leak.

### 7. The low walls

![The low wall covered, top included](step-07.jpg)

The coating runs up the low walls and over their tops too.

### 8. The result

![The roof with its low walls coated white](step-08.jpg)

Channels, low walls and outlets all covered, with no open joints.
