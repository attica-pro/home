### 1. Dirt in the corners

![A blackened corner next to the balcony door sill](step-01.jpg)

Dirt and black marks from standing water had built up in the corners and along the balcony door sill.

### 2. Stained tiles

![Balcony tiles with stains and black grout lines](step-02.jpg)

The grout had turned black and the tiles were covered in stains. Anything left on the surface stops the new material from bonding.

### 3. A clean base

![The tiles clean and dry](step-03.jpg)

The surface is cleaned thoroughly and left to dry before anything goes on.

### 4. First coat

![The first white coat over the tiles](step-04.jpg)

The white waterproof coating goes straight over the tiles. Nothing has to be lifted, so there is no dust or rubble in the home.

### 5. A smooth, glossy surface

![Close-up of the finished glossy surface](step-05.jpg)

The coating closes up the grout lines and leaves a smooth surface that is easy to clean.

### 6. Corners and skirting

![The coating turned up the wall like a skirting](step-06.jpg)

The material is taken a little way up the wall like a skirting, so the joint between floor and wall is not left open.
