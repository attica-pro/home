### 1. Before we started

![The living room before the work, seen through the glass doors](step-01.jpg)

The room is cleared and things are moved aside so we can work freely.

### 2. Bricking it up

![The wall opening being filled with bricks](step-02.jpg)

The opening is closed with bricks, course by course, in line with the rest of the wall.

### 3. Up to the ceiling

![The brickwork reaching the ceiling](step-03.jpg)

The last courses are wedged in under the ceiling so the new wall ties in with the old one.

### 4. Plaster

![The first coat of plaster over the bricks](step-04.jpg)

The first coat of plaster covers the bricks and laps a little onto the surrounding wall.

### 5. Second coat

![The wall with its second coat of plaster](step-05.jpg)

Mesh goes over the joints with the old wall so cracks do not open there. The second coat brings the surface flush with the rest of the wall.

### 6. Filler

![The wall skimmed with filler, edges still visible](step-06.jpg)

Filler gives a smooth surface and blends the edges between the new and the old.

### 7. Painting

![The painted wall, with no sign of the opening](step-07.jpg)

Once painted, there is no telling where the opening used to be.

### 8. The result

![The finished, furnished living room](step-08.jpg)

The living room is set up again, with fresh paint and a wall that shows no trace of the old opening.
