### 1. Where we started

![The old stained coating along the low wall](step-01.jpg)

The old coating had faded and was covered in stains, especially along the low wall.

### 2. The whole surface

![The terrace before the work](step-02.jpg)

The wear covered the whole terrace, not just a few spots.

### 3. A clean base

![The cleaned terrace with the primer tins](step-03.jpg)

The surface is cleaned so the new material bonds to a sound base.

### 4. Primer

![Brushing on the primer](step-04.jpg)

Primer goes over the whole surface. It binds any remaining dust and helps the waterproof coating stick.

### 5. Ready for waterproofing

![The tub of waterproof coating on the primed terrace](step-05.jpg)

Once the primer is dry, the terrace is ready for the main coat.

### 6. Low walls first

![The low walls under the railings coated white](step-06.jpg)

The coating goes on the low walls and the corners under the railings first, as these are the trickiest spots.

### 7. The white coat

![The white coat on the floor and up the corner of the low wall](step-07.jpg)

The white membrane covers the floor and joins up with the low walls, so no joint is left open.

### 8. The result

![The finished terrace, white right up to the railings](step-08.jpg)

One continuous white surface from edge to edge. The white also reflects the sun, so the rooms below heat up less.
