### 1. Where we started

![The terrace before the work](step-01.jpg)

The surface was worn out, with peeling patches of old coatings and cracks that let water through.

### 2. Pressure washing

![Pressure washing the terrace](step-02.jpg)

First the dust, loose material and leftovers of old coatings come off, so the new material bonds to a clean base.

### 3. Primer

![Cans of primer on the terrace](step-03.jpg)

The primer binds any remaining dust and helps the membrane stick properly to the concrete.

### 4. Sealing the cracks

![Cracks sealed with white filler](step-04.jpg)

Every crack is filled on its own before the main coat goes on. These are usually the places where water gets in.

### 5. First coat

![The first coat of white membrane](step-05.jpg)

The white liquid membrane goes over the whole surface and up the low walls, so there is no open joint at the corners.

### 6. Second coat

![The second coat, with the tin of material](step-06.jpg)

The second coat builds up the final thickness and covers anything the first one missed.

### 7. The result

![The finished terrace](step-07.jpg)

One continuous white surface with no joints. The white also reflects the sun, so the flat below stays cooler in summer.
