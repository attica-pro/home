### 1. The wall before

![The outside wall on the balcony before the insulation](step-01.jpg)

The wall had nothing but its old render. Without insulation, heat and cold from outside pass straight into the rooms.

### 2. The first boards

![Fitting an insulation board at the bottom of the wall](step-02.jpg)

The boards are glued to the wall starting from the bottom, one next to the other, with no gaps between them.

### 3. The whole wall covered

![The wall covered with white insulation boards](step-03.jpg)

The boards cover the whole surface. Around doors and windows they are cut to fit.

### 4. Fixing with anchors

![Anchors fixing the boards to the wall](step-04.jpg)

On top of the adhesive, every board is also held by special anchors driven into the wall.

### 5. Covering the anchors

![Anchor heads covered over, around the window](step-05.jpg)

The anchor heads and the joints are filled over, so the surface is even before the render goes on.

### 6. Render

![The wall with its first coat of render](step-06.jpg)

A coat of render is spread over all the boards to protect them.

### 7. Finishing

![Finishing the wall by hand](step-07.jpg)

The final coat is worked by hand so the corners and edges come out straight.

### 8. The result

![The finished wall next to the balcony door](step-08.jpg)

A smooth white wall. Insulation on the outside keeps the rooms cooler in summer and warmer in winter.
