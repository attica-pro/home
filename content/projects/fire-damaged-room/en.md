### 1. After the fire

![The room blackened by the fire](step-01.jpg)

Soot covered the walls and ceiling, and the heat had loosened plaster and paint.

### 2. Assessing the damage

![Burnt walls and fallen plaster](step-02.jpg)

Before anything else we check what is still sound and what has to go. Anything the heat has loosened comes off.

### 3. Cleaning the ceiling

![The ceiling during cleaning](step-03.jpg)

The soot is cleaned off section by section. Paint over it and the stain comes back through the new paint.

### 4. Repairs and primer

![The room after the repairs](step-04.jpg)

The plaster is repaired and a stain-blocking primer goes on, so the final paint stays clean.

### 5. Painting

![Walls painted again](step-05.jpg)

Two coats of paint on the walls and ceiling.

### 6. The result

![The finished room, with the light fitting back in place](step-06.jpg)

A bright room, ready to be used again.
