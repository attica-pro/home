### 1. The damage

![A skylight with a broken base and debris around it](step-01.jpg)

The skylight base had cracked and pieces of the old material had come loose. Water gets in easily at spots like this.

### 2. Breaking out

![Loose material broken out around the base](step-02.jpg)

Anything that had decayed around the base is broken out and removed, so we work on a sound surface.

### 3. First repair

![The cleaned skylight base with its first white coat](step-03.jpg)

The base is repaired and gets its first coat of waterproofing.

### 4. Both skylights

![The two skylights on the roof](step-04.jpg)

The second skylight goes through the same process so no weak spot is left.

### 5. Waterproofing

![The skylight bases covered in white waterproofing](step-05.jpg)

The white coating covers the bases and runs out onto the roof around them, closing the joint with the floor.

### 6. The result

![The sealed skylights](step-06.jpg)

The bases are now continuous and sealed, with no cracks or open joints.
