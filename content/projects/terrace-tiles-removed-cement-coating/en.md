### 1. The old tiles

![Broken tiles pulled up on the terrace](step-01.jpg)

The tiles had cracked and come loose. They all come up so we can see what is underneath.

### 2. Underneath

![The old dark layer with a rotten wooden joint](step-02.jpg)

With the tiles gone, the old dark layer showed, and in one joint a strip of wood that had rotted.

### 3. The joint

![The opened joint with the rotten wood inside](step-03.jpg)

The wood in the joint had rotted and left gaps. The joint is opened up and cleaned out before it is sealed.

### 4. The materials

![Bags of powder material and a bottle of liquid on the terrace](step-04.jpg)

The materials for the new layer wait on the cleaned surface.

### 5. Sealing along the length

![A joint in the floor sealed along its length](step-05.jpg)

The crack running along the floor is filled with sealant, because that is where water finds its way in.

### 6. The wall junction

![The narrow balcony with fresh material at the foot of the wall](step-06.jpg)

The joint between floor and wall gets its own treatment. Two surfaces meet there, and cracks open easily.

### 7. The narrow balcony

![The narrow balcony with its new grey coating](step-07.jpg)

The new layer runs the full length of the balcony, right up to the door.

### 8. The result

![The terrace with its new coating, still drying](step-08.jpg)

The terrace and balcony now have one continuous surface, with no broken tiles and no open joints.
