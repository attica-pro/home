### 1. Stripped to the brick

![Bare brick walls and the frame for a concealed cistern](step-01.jpg)

Tiles and plaster come off down to the brick. A frame for a wall-hung toilet with a concealed cistern goes on the wall, which saves space in such a narrow room.

### 2. Plastering

![Plaster being spread over the brick](step-02.jpg)

Plaster is spread over the brick and covers the new pipework.

### 3. Plastered walls

![The WC with every wall plastered and the supply points sticking out](step-03.jpg)

Every wall is now plastered, with the water points sticking out where the fixtures will go.

### 4. Smooth walls

![Smooth white walls with the supply points in place](step-04.jpg)

The walls are smoothed so the final surface comes out straight and clean.

### 5. Floor tiles

![New grey tiles being laid on the floor](step-05.jpg)

The large grey tiles are laid around the floor drain, with spacers that keep the joints straight.
