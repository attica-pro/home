### 1. Protecting the furniture

![A room with the furniture covered in plastic sheeting](step-01.jpg)

Before the first tin of paint is opened, the furniture and floor are covered in plastic sheeting. That keeps the home clean for the whole job.

### 2. Filling

![A brown wall with filler in the holes](step-02.jpg)

The brown wall had holes from screws and hooks. Every mark is filled so it will not show through the new paint.

### 3. Grey walls

![A room with grey walls and a chandelier, furniture still covered](step-03.jpg)

In another room the walls take on a soft grey. The chandelier and furniture stay covered until the end.

### 4. The feature wall

![A dark grey wall next to light-coloured walls](step-04.jpg)

The brown wall became dark grey and the walls around it light. One wall in a different colour is enough to give a room character.
