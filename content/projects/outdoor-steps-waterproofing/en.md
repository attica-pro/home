### 1. Before

![The old outdoor steps](step-01.jpg)

The steps had been worn down by the weather and by foot traffic. On an outdoor staircase, rainwater easily finds its way in through the joints and edges.

### 2. Preparation

![Tools and a bucket of material on the steps](step-02.jpg)

Anything loose or worn is taken off and the surface is cleaned, so the new layers sit on a sound base.

### 3. The first layer

![The steps with a light-coloured layer](step-03.jpg)

The first layer goes over every tread and riser.

### 4. The next layer

![The steps and floor with a dark grey layer](step-04.jpg)

Next comes a dark grey layer over the whole staircase and the floor in front of it, building up the final thickness.

### 5. The edges

![Tools on the steps during the last stage of work](step-05.jpg)

The edges and corners of the steps need extra care, as that's where foot traffic wears a coating first. That way the staircase is left with no open joints for rainwater.
