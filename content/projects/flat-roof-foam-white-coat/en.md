### 1. Spraying the foam

![Spraying polyurethane foam, with plastic sheeting protecting the wall](step-01.jpg)

The foam is sprayed across the roof. The walls around are covered with plastic sheeting to keep them clean.

### 2. The foam layer

![The roof covered in foam, with the church behind](step-02.jpg)

The foam forms one continuous insulating layer with no joints, and goes around the steel bars sticking up from the slab.

### 3. White protection

![The white coating going over the foam](step-03.jpg)

Foam shouldn't be left exposed to the sun, so a white coating goes on to protect it.

### 4. Over the whole surface

![Applying the white coating on the roof](step-04.jpg)

The coating goes over the whole roof. The white reflects the sun and keeps the space below cooler.
