### 1. Before

![A sidewalk with moss, cracks and weeds](step-01.jpg)

The slabs were broken, with weeds and moss growing between them. A sidewalk like this is easy to slip or trip on.

### 2. New concrete

![Fresh concrete with a timber board across it for protection](step-02.jpg)

The sidewalk is laid with new concrete around the drain cover. A timber board blocks the way while the concrete sets.

### 3. All the way along

![The new concrete sidewalk along the street](step-03.jpg)

The new concrete runs the full length, level from the kerb to the wall.

### 4. Painting

![The sidewalk painted grey](step-04.jpg)

Once the concrete has dried, the surface is painted grey and the base of the wall is painted white.

### 5. The result

![The finished sidewalk from above](step-05.jpg)

A clean, continuous sidewalk with no broken slabs or weeds.
