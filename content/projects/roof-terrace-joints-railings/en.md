### 1. A crumbling surface

![Broken coating at the foot of the railings](step-01.jpg)

At the foot of the railings the old coating had broken up and the material underneath was crumbling.

### 2. The parapet

![The flaking top of a parapet](step-02.jpg)

On top of the parapet, too, the render had flaked away and was letting water into the wall.

### 3. The roof before

![The old, stained roof surface](step-03.jpg)

An old, stained surface, divided into squares by joints.

### 4. Corners first

![White waterproofing in the corner between floor and parapet](step-04.jpg)

The joint between floor and parapet is covered with white waterproofing that rounds off the corner and runs up the wall.

### 5. The joints

![White strips over the joints across the roof](step-05.jpg)

The joints across the roof are sealed with white strips, because that is where the surface moves and opens up.

### 6. Around obstacles

![White coating around the base of a satellite dish](step-06.jpg)

The base of the satellite dish and the corners around it are covered with no gaps.

### 7. Along the railings

![A white strip along the railings meeting a joint](step-07.jpg)

The white strip runs all the way round the edge and links up with the joints.

### 8. The result

![The roof with a white perimeter and sealed joints](step-08.jpg)

Perimeter and joints sealed. The places where water used to get in are now closed.
