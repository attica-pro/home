### 1. Where we started

![Two drain outlets with cracked material around them](step-01.jpg)

The material around the drains had cracked. These are among the most sensitive spots, because all the water collects there.

### 2. Breaking out

![Loose material broken out around the drains](step-02.jpg)

Anything that isn't holding is broken out and removed, and the drains are sealed around their edges.

### 3. A waterproof patch

![White waterproofing around the drains](step-03.jpg)

A white waterproof patch covers the whole area around the drains.

### 4. Beyond the damage

![The dried patch with the drains left open](step-04.jpg)

The patch extends beyond the damage so it also grips the sound surface around it.

### 5. The result

![The drains after the final coat](step-05.jpg)

A final coat matching the floor covers the patch. The drains stay open and water runs away without finding any cracks.
