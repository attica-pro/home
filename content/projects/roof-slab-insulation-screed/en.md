### 1. The slab before

![The bare roof slab with steel bars sticking up](step-01.jpg)

The roof was bare concrete with no protection from rain or sun.

### 2. Foam along the edges

![A worker spraying white foam along the edge of the roof](step-02.jpg)

Spray polyurethane foam goes along the edge, next to the wall. It fills the corners and joints, which are the weakest spots.

### 3. Tar

![Glossy black tar on the slab](step-03.jpg)

Tar is spread over the whole slab and seals the pores of the concrete.

### 4. The whole surface covered

![The whole roof covered in tar](step-04.jpg)

The tar runs up to the low walls and around the steel bars, so no spot is left bare under the screed.

### 5. Laying the screed

![A worker spreading and levelling the screed with a vibrating screed board](step-05.jpg)

The screed is spread over the tar and levelled with a vibrating screed board, which also compacts it.

### 6. A smooth surface

![Fresh screed across the whole roof](step-06.jpg)

The screed covers the whole roof with no hollows, so rainwater can run off.

### 7. The result

![The roof finished with its new screed](step-07.jpg)

The slab now has protection underneath and a solid, level surface on top.
