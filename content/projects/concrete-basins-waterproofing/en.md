### 1. The bare pit

![A concrete inspection pit with pipes before waterproofing](step-01.jpg)

The inspection pit was bare concrete, with pipes coming in through the sides. Concrete on its own soaks up water.

### 2. First coat

![Grey waterproofing on the floor and sides](step-02.jpg)

The first coat of waterproofing goes over the floor and sides and around every pipe.

### 3. The channel

![The narrow channel with its first coat](step-03.jpg)

The same is done in the narrow channel, right up to its edges.

### 4. White coat

![The square pit with a white coat](step-04.jpg)

A white coat follows over the grey one, covering the rim of the pit as well.

### 5. The long pit

![The long pit with a white coat](step-05.jpg)

The long pit gets its white coat too, worked carefully around the upright pipe.

### 6. Channel and pit

![The channel and the pit, waterproofed](step-06.jpg)

The channel and the pit at its end are now one continuous surface, with no open joints.

### 7. The result

![The finished long pit with a glossy surface](step-07.jpg)

A smooth, glossy surface that doesn't let water leak through and is easy to clean.
