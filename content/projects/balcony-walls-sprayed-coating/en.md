### 1. Covering up

![A balcony door and awning wrapped in plastic](step-01.jpg)

Before spraying, doors, windows and awnings are covered. The foam travels as a fine cloud, and anything left uncovered gets marked.

### 2. Spraying

![Spraying the wall from the balcony](step-02.jpg)

Spray polyurethane foam goes onto the whole wall from a spray gun and expands into one continuous insulating layer.

### 3. Section by section

![The balcony wall partway through spraying](step-03.jpg)

The wall is covered section by section while the balcony door stays wrapped.

### 4. Around the windows

![The foam around a window and an air conditioning pipe](step-04.jpg)

The foam goes right up to the window frames and over the air conditioning pipe on the wall, leaving no gap behind it.

### 5. The texture

![The slightly lumpy foam surface next to a window](step-05.jpg)

The foam surface comes out slightly lumpy. It is one continuous layer, with no joints for heat or cold to get through.

### 6. The result

![The finished balcony wall](step-06.jpg)

The balcony walls are insulated from end to end, with no marks on the frames or the awnings.
