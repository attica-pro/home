### 1. The roof before

![The red metal roof with faded paint](step-01.jpg)

The sheets had faded and the flashing where the roof meets the wall was worn.

### 2. The wall above the flashing

![A broken spot in the wall above the flashing](step-02.jpg)

Where the roof meets the wall, the render had opened up. Water finds its way under the roof through spots like this.

### 3. Crumbling foam

![Old foam crumbling inside the joint](step-03.jpg)

The old foam that filled the joint had broken down in the sun and left openings.

### 4. The gap at the parapet

![A gap between the roof sheet and the parapet](step-04.jpg)

There was an open gap between the sheet and the parapet, and the parapet had turned black with damp.

### 5. Cleaning

![The channel along the parapet, cleaned out](step-05.jpg)

The channel next to the parapet is cleared of dirt and debris so the new materials bond properly.

### 6. New foam

![New foam filling the joint](step-06.jpg)

The joint is filled with new foam, closing it along its whole length.

### 7. Sealing and screws

![The joint sealed and the screws covered](step-07.jpg)

A waterproof coating goes over the foam to protect it from the sun. Every screw is covered too, because water often gets in through the screw holes.

### 8. The result

![The roof from above, with every screw covered](step-08.jpg)

The joint at the parapet is closed and the screws are sealed across the whole roof.
