### 1. The roof before

![Corrugated sheets covered in moss and lichen](step-01.jpg)

The sheets had darkened and were covered in moss and lichen, which hold moisture on the surface.

### 2. Up close

![Lichen on the ridges of the sheets](step-02.jpg)

On the ridges of the corrugations the dirt had built up into a thick layer.

### 3. Cleaning

![Washing the roof](step-03.jpg)

The roof is washed until the dirt and lichen are gone. The coating only bonds to a clean surface.

### 4. First coat

![The first coat, with the tin of material](step-04.jpg)

The coating goes on the ridges and the joints between sheets first, then over the whole surface.

### 5. The difference

![Coated and uncoated parts of the roof side by side](step-05.jpg)

The difference is easy to see here: the finished part at the top and right, the old surface at the bottom left.

### 6. Right to the edges

![The coating running up to the wall](step-06.jpg)

The coating goes right up to the wall and around every obstacle, so no spot is left bare.

### 7. The result

![The finished roof](step-07.jpg)

One even, bright surface that reflects the sun and protects the sheets from the rain.
