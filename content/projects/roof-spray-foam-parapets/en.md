### 1. One layer over the whole roof

![The roof covered in polyurethane foam](step-01.jpg)

The foam is sprayed over the entire surface and forms one continuous layer with no joints. It keeps out heat and cold and leaves no seams for water to find.

### 2. Around the air conditioning units

![Foam around the base of an air conditioning unit and its pipes](step-02.jpg)

Equipment bases and pipes crossing the roof are usually the weak points. The foam wraps around them on every side.

### 3. Up the walls

![The foam running from the floor up the wall](step-03.jpg)

At the corner between floor and wall the layer doesn't stop. It carries on up the wall, so there is no open joint.

### 4. The edges

![The edge of the slab covered in foam](step-04.jpg)

The foam also covers the edges of the slab, so the insulation doesn't stop short at the rim.

### 5. The result

![The finished roof](step-05.jpg)

One insulated surface from edge to edge. The flat below is shielded from the heat in summer and the cold in winter.
