### 1. Where we started

![An old cistern and peeling walls above the tiles](step-01.jpg)

Above the tiles, damp had lifted the plaster and paint, and the old cistern had rusted.

### 2. Stripping back

![A damp-stained wall after the old fittings were removed](step-02.jpg)

The old fittings come out and anything loose is scraped off the wall, so the repair starts from a sound base.

### 3. Repairs and paint

![A smooth white wall and ceiling](step-03.jpg)

The surfaces are smoothed and painted white. Only the holes for the new fittings remain.

### 4. The result

![The bathroom with white walls above the tiles](step-04.jpg)

The tiles stayed, and above them the walls are now clean and bright.
